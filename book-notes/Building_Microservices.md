# Building Microservices

### Preface
- Microservices promote fine-grained services that can be changed, deployed, and released independently.
- Ideal for organizations moving toward loosely coupled systems with autonomous teams.
- Microservices provide flexibility in building systems but come with significant complexity, especially for distributed systems.

### Chapter 1: What Are Microservices?
- Microservices have become increasingly popular.
- The chapter distills the essence of microservice architecture.
- It provides a broad overview of the impact on various aspects of software delivery.

- Highlights characteristics of well-behaved microservices to ensure system manageability.
